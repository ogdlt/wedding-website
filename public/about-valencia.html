01 · Where Olimpia grew up

Rocafort

The town just north of Valencia where Olimpia grew up — surrounded by orange groves, quiet streets and the landscape of the Valencian huerta.

02 · Where we will get married

Masía Xamandreu

Where we will get married. A countryside estate outside Valencia, surrounded by palm trees, orange trees, cypresses, golden stone and the Mediterranean light we love so much.

03 · The heart of Valencia

City Centre

The historic centre of Valencia, with its old streets, plazas, churches, markets and terraces. This is the Valencia of wandering, stopping for coffee, and discovering the city slowly.

04 · By the sea

Cabanyal

Historically the fishermen's area, today it is an integral part of the city — colourful, lively and closely tied to Valencia's Mediterranean identity.

05 · Rice country

El Saler & L'Albufera

The landscape south of the city, where pine forests, dunes, rice fields and the Albufera lagoon meet. It is the birthplace of paella valenciana and one of the most special corners of Valencia.

Discover the city

Things to do

A few of our favourite ways to explore Valencia

01

Plaza del Ayuntamiento

Begin in the heart of Valencia at the City Hall square, framed by elegant architecture, fountains and some of the city's most beautiful historic buildings.

02

Mercado Central

Wander through the beautiful local market, with its fresh fish, Valencian produce, colourful stalls and remarkable modernist architecture. While you are there, try a fresh horchata with fartons, a classic Valencian treat.

03

La Lonja de la Seda

Step inside Valencia's historic Silk Exchange, a Gothic landmark just across from the market and one of the city's most treasured buildings.

04

Cathedral & El Miguelete

Discover the cathedral and climb the Miguelete bell tower for sweeping views across the old town's rooftops, church domes and plazas.

05

Plaza de la Virgen

Pause in one of Valencia's prettiest old-town squares, surrounded by the cathedral, the basilica and lively terraces for a coffee in the sun.

06

Oceanogràfic

Visit the striking aquarium in the City of Arts and Sciences, then take a stroll around the futuristic buildings and the nearby Turia Gardens.
